Features

What the finished product is meant to include.

Always on

Calls are answered day and night, on weekends and holidays, with no time spent on hold.

Six languages

The receptionist talks with each caller in the caller's own language.

{% for language in ["English", "Hinglish", "Hindi", "Telugu", "Tamil", "Spanish"] %} {{ language }} {% endfor %}

Appointment booking

New bookings, changes and cancellations are handled during the call, against your calendar.

SMS confirmations

The caller receives the appointment details by text message.

Call summaries

Each call is turned into a short checklist of what was done, so you can see what happened at a glance.

Transcripts

The full conversation is kept in writing, in the language it was spoken in.
