About {{ app_name }}

A phone receptionist for small businesses whose customers do not all speak the same language.

Why it exists

In a small business, the person who answers the phone is usually busy with something else: a patient, a customer at the counter, a job on site. Calls that come in then, or after closing time, go to voicemail. And some callers would rather speak Hindi, Telugu, Tamil or Spanish than English.

What we are building

An AI receptionist that picks up every call, talks with the caller in English, Hinglish, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil or Spanish, books the appointment, and sends a text message to confirm it. The business owner sees every call, with a summary and a transcript, in a dashboard.

Where we are

{{ app_name }} is at an early stage. The owner dashboard works with sample calls. Answering real phone calls, booking into a real calendar and sending text messages are not built yet. The How it works page marks each step as built or planned.
