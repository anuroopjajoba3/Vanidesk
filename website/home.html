What it is being built to do

Answer every call, day and night

The receptionist picks up as soon as the phone rings. No hold music, no voicemail, also after closing time.

Speak six languages

The caller talks the way they talk at home, and the receptionist answers in the same language.

{% for language in ["English", "Hinglish", "Hindi", "Telugu", "Tamil", "Spanish"] %} {{ language }} {% endfor %}

Book appointments

It looks for a free slot in your calendar, then books, moves or cancels the appointment during the call.

Text a confirmation

After the call, the caller gets an SMS with the date and time, so nobody has to write it down.

Show you every call

The owner dashboard lists each call with a short summary, the appointments that came out of it, and the full transcript.
